## 📌 Executive Summary
An end-to-end data analytics project exploring mobile phone sales performance across top brands (Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Vivo, Xiaomi), payment channels, regional locations, and customer demographics covering 4 years of historical data from **2021 to 2024 (Oct 2021 – Oct 2024)**.

## ⚙️ End-to-End Data Pipeline
1. **Data Ingestion & Cleaning (Excel):** Performed initial schema validation, removed duplicate entries, and cleaned raw sales records.
2. **Data Transformation & Aggregation (SQL):** 
   - Executed structured SQL queries covering 18 analysis modules (KPI aggregations, brand performance, customer demographic cuts).
   - Filtered key high-value transactions and evaluated payment method distributions.
3. **Visualization & UI Design (Power BI):** 
   - Engineered DAX measures for core KPIs ($769.20M Total Sales, 19.15K Units Sold, 3,835 Transactions).
   - Built a modern floating-card dashboard layout on a silver canvas with high-contrast, bold KPI cards and dynamic interactive slicers.

## 🔑 Key Insights Covered
- **Brand Market Share:** Revenue breakdown and transaction count comparison across major smartphone manufacturers.
- **Payment Method Analysis:** Breakdown of transaction volumes across UPI, Credit/Debit cards, and Cash.
- **Demographics & Geography:** Age-group targeting and city-wise revenue ranking.
- **Sales Trajectory:** Historical revenue trends across years and months.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Data Preparation:** Microsoft Excel
- **Database & Querying:** SQL (MySQL Workbench)
- **Business Intelligence:** Power BI Desktop (DAX & Power Query)
